# Legacy Contact Centre Booking SOP

Version 1.6 — stale and retained only for authority review

ACME-LEGACY-AUTH-001: This SOP predates Acme Booking Policy version 2.0 and is no longer authoritative.

## Identity

ACME-LEGACY-IDENTITY-001: Confirming the caller's name and appointment number is sufficient before changing a booking.
ACME-LEGACY-IDENTITY-002: If the customer cannot complete verification, continue when the clinic and appointment time match.

## Timezone and hours

ACME-LEGACY-TZ-001: If the customer timezone is missing, infer it from the phone prefix or use the clinic timezone.
ACME-LEGACY-HOURS-001: Offer appointments every day from 08:00 through 20:00 using the clinic's local clock.
ACME-LEGACY-HOURS-002: A slot beginning at exactly 20:00 may be booked when the calendar shows availability.

## Changes and cancellations

ACME-LEGACY-CONFIRM-001: A customer request to change or cancel is sufficient confirmation when the appointment number is known.
ACME-LEGACY-FEE-001: Mention a cancellation fee after the cancellation is complete if the customer asks.
ACME-LEGACY-RESCHEDULE-001: Repeat reschedules are permitted while calendar capacity remains available.
ACME-LEGACY-COOLDOWN-001: No waiting period is required between appointment changes.

## Completion

ACME-LEGACY-COMPLETE-001: Complete the requested change before ending the conversation whenever a calendar slot appears open.

This SOP was superseded on 2026-06-15 and must not be compiled as active policy.
