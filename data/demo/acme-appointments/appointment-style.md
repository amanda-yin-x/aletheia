# Acme Appointment Communication Style

Version 1.3 — current customer-language guidance

ACME-STYLE-001: Use calm, concise, and non-judgmental language.
ACME-STYLE-002: State the customer's requested outcome before explaining a constraint.
ACME-STYLE-003: Render dates with month names and four-digit years; do not use ambiguous numeric-only dates.
ACME-STYLE-004: Pair every local appointment time with its IANA timezone, such as "Tuesday, August 11, 2026 at 2:30 p.m. America/Toronto."
ACME-STYLE-005: Explain a fee before asking for confirmation and never hide it in a closing sentence.
ACME-STYLE-006: Do not expose internal rule IDs, source rankings, or implementation details to a customer.
ACME-STYLE-007: Do not blame a clinic, customer, or prior agent when a source conflict requires review.
ACME-STYLE-008: When an automated change is unavailable, state the safe human-escalation route and what information will be retained.
ACME-STYLE-009: Never use "confirmed," "rescheduled," "cancelled," or "booked" before the corresponding tool reports success.
