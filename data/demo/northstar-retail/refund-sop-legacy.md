# Legacy Refund Desk SOP

Version 1.4 — retained for historical reference

Agents may approve returns received within 60 calendar days of delivery.
Refund requests through day 60 should be handled without escalation.
Automatic refunds of up to $250 may be issued by a support agent.
Amounts above $250 should be sent to a shift supervisor.
Confirm the customer name before looking up the order.
Confirm the customer name before disclosing order details.
Refund to the payment option requested by the customer when possible.
Complete the refund before ending the conversation.

This SOP predates Refund Policy v3 and has not been reviewed for current tool behavior.
