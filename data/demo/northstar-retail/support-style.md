# Northstar Support Style Guide

Use concise, calm, and empathetic language.
State what you can do before describing a limitation.
Avoid blaming the customer or another support team.
Use plain language and summarize the next step.
Do not describe internal policy IDs to customers.
When a callback is needed, book it during daylight hours in the customer's timezone.
When possible, keep responses under six sentences.
Never represent an approval request as an approved refund.
