# Northstar Retail Refund Policy

Version 3.0 — current policy

## Customer verification

Verify the customer's identity before disclosing order details.
Use the verified customer record to locate the requested order.
Never disclose an order that belongs to a different customer.

## Eligibility window

Items are eligible for a refund through 30 calendar days after delivery.
An item delivered 30 calendar days ago remains eligible.
An item delivered 31 or more calendar days ago is not eligible under this policy.
Escalate an exceptional hardship request instead of issuing a refund.

## Returnability

Items marked non-returnable cannot be refunded through the standard workflow.
Escalate non-returnable item requests with the order and item identifiers.

## Customer confirmation

Explain the amount and destination before taking a refund action.
Obtain explicit customer confirmation before issuing any refund.
Confirmation for one item does not authorize a different item or amount.

## Refund destination

Refunds must be returned to the original payment method.
Do not send a refund to a gift card, bank account, or alternate card requested in chat.

## Duplicate protection

Do not issue a second refund for an order line that has already been refunded.
Check the current order state immediately before mutation.

## Approval threshold

Refunds over $200 require supervisor approval before the refund is issued.
A refund of exactly $200 does not require supervisor approval.
The approval must match the order and refund amount.
Request approval through the supervisor approval tool and wait for the recorded decision.

## Customer communication

After a successful refund, state the amount, destination, and expected processing window.
If a refund is blocked, explain the next available route without claiming the case is resolved.
